# Boat Chase

Paddle your boat across an endless sea. Enemies chase you, mines and ice lie in your way, and the longer you survive the more trouble comes. Collect coins, grab power-ups, spend coins in the shop, and beat your best score.

## Everything in the game at a glance

- **Endless sea** with islands, ice, plastic, mines and coins.
- **7 enemy types** and **2 bosses**.
- **5 power-ups**: shield, speed, magnet, slow-mo and bomb.
- **Dash** to dodge danger.
- **3 boats** and **4 upgrades** in the shop.
- **Hazards**: islands, floating mines, slippery ice, floating plastic, a big wave, storms with lightning, fog.
- **Sea life**: tourist boats, fishing boats, cruise ships, anchored boats, water skiers and schools of fish.
- **Day and night**, rain, fog and storms.
- **Surge difficulty**: waves of trouble that get bigger over time.
- **Sound and music**, settings, pause, and this guide.

## How to play

Stay alive and collect coins. Your **score = seconds survived + coins collected** in the run. Your best score is saved.

You wreck if you touch an island, a mine, a boat, an enemy or a shell. A **shield** absorbs one hit, and a **dash** makes you briefly untouchable (except against islands).

The trick is to make enemies wreck themselves: lead them into islands, mines, boats and each other. Killing gives no points, but it clears the way.

### Controls

| Action | Phone / touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | Hold and drag: the boat turns toward your finger | `←` `→` or `A` `D` |
| Dash | DASH button (bottom right) | `Space` or `Shift` |
| Pause / resume | Pause button (top right) | `P` or `Esc` |
| Start / retry | PLAY and Retry buttons | `Enter` or `Space` on a menu |

The game pauses by itself when you leave the app or switch windows. The screen shows SCORE, BEST, TIME and COINS at the top left, with a timer for each active power-up under them.

## The sea (hazards)

| Thing | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Islands** | Solid sand islands. Touching one wrecks you. Some drift slowly. |
| **Floating mines** (red lights) | Touching one wrecks you. Their blast kills enemies nearby and sets off other mines in a chain. |
| **Ice sheets** | Pale patches of ice. They are not solid, but **slippery**: on ice your boat loses grip and keeps sliding the way it was going, however you steer. Steer before you get there and ride it out. |
| **Floating plastic** | Bottles, bags and cans. Sailing through a patch slows your boat by about 45% (a dash ignores it). Enemies are not slowed. |
| **Coin trails** | Curved lines of coins. Collecting them is your score and your money. |
| **Big wave** | Every 40 to 70 seconds. The edge it comes from flashes with arrows for about 3 seconds, then a band of foam sweeps across and pushes you and enemies along. It does not kill, but it can push you into rocks and mines. |
| **Storms** | Warning circles appear, then lightning strikes. It wrecks you if you stand in one, and kills normal enemies caught in it. |
| **Fog** | Hides everything outside a circle around your boat. |
| **Night** | After sunset the screen is dark apart from a lit circle around your boat. |

## Other boats and sea life

These are not enemies, but **hitting any boat wrecks you** (a shield or dash saves you). Enemies that crash into them explode, so they make good cover. Bosses ignore them.

- **Tourist boats**: small coloured boats with waving tourists.
- **Fishing boats**: smaller, faster boats with a mast.
- **Cruise ships**: huge and slow, solid along their whole length.
- **Anchored boats**: fishing boats that stay put.
- **Water skiers**: a speedboat towing a skier on a rope. Avoid both the boat and the skier.
- **Schools of fish**: they scatter when you come close and regroup after you leave. Just for show.
- **Gulls**: birds flying overhead.

## Enemies

Enemies spawn just off-screen and chase you. Arrows at the screen edge point to ones you cannot see (orange for shooters and mine layers, large pink for bosses).

| Enemy | Appears at | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Torpedo** | start | Fast homing torpedo. Disappears after about 9 seconds. |
| **Drifting mine** | 14 s | Slow chasing mine with a blinking red light. |
| **Gunboat** | 22 s | Circles you at range and fires shells every 2.2 seconds. |
| **Shark** | 30 s | Very fast with sharp turns. |
| **Torpedo boat** | 35 s | Navy ship that keeps its distance and fires homing torpedoes: one every 3.2 s, two at a time after 90 s. |
| **Dasher** | 40 s | Swordfish. It stops, shows a flashing red line, then charges along it at high speed. |
| **Mine layer** | 55 s | Circles you and drops hostile mines (orange lights) behind it. |

Enemies die when they hit islands, mines, boats or each other, and to lightning and the bomb.

### Bosses

The first boss arrives at about 100 seconds, and the next one about 110 seconds after you defeat it. They alternate, get more health each time, and show a health bar at the top. The music changes in a boss fight, and a defeated boss drops a burst of coins.

- **Battleship**: keeps its distance, fires a fan of three shells and launches two torpedoes every few seconds. It fires faster when below half health.
- **Kraken**: chases you slowly, slams the water in a flashing circle on your position, and releases drifting mines. It slams faster below half health.

Bosses take damage from mine blasts (2), the bomb (4) and enemies crashing into them (1). They ignore other boats.

## Difficulty

Difficulty comes in **surges**. Nothing ever gets faster: your boat and the enemies keep the same speed all game. Only the number of enemies changes.

- Every 45 seconds the trouble rises to a peak, then drops back to a calm.
- Each peak is bigger than the one before: the first is small, and later ones bring enemies every half second, often in pairs.
- A calm is quiet (about one enemy every 4 seconds). Use it to collect coins and power-ups.
- New enemy types, bosses and storms arrive on their own timers (see the tables).

## Your abilities

- **Dash**: a short burst of speed (about 0.35 seconds) that makes you untouchable by enemies, shells and mines. It recharges in 3 seconds (the ring around the button shows progress).
- **Shield**: absorbs one hit, then gives about 0.8 seconds of grace.

## Power-ups

Glowing rings that appear near you and fade after about 14 seconds.

| Power-up | Icon | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | 🛡 | Absorbs one hit. Lasts 6 seconds plus upgrades. |
| **Speed** | ⚡ | Your boat is 40% faster for 8 seconds. |
| **Magnet** | 🧲 | Pulls coins and power-ups from three times as far for 10 seconds. |
| **Slow-mo** | ⏱ | Enemies, shells and warnings run at half speed for 6 seconds. |
| **Bomb** | 💣 | Instantly destroys everything within range (damages a boss). |

## Coins and the shop

Coins from a run are added to your total when it ends. Open the **Shop** from the main menu or the game-over screen.

| Boat | Cost | Speed | Turning | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kayak** | free | 30 | 3.4 | Balanced, tight turns |
| **Speedboat** | 150 | 38 | 2.5 | Fast, wide turns |
| **Jet Ski** | 300 | 34 | 3.0 | Dash recharges in 1.9 s instead of 3 s |

| Upgrade | Each level | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield time** | +1 second of shield | 5 |
| **Handling** | +6% turn rate | 5 |
| **Coin magnet** | Pulls coins from further away | 5 |
| **Dash recharge** | 12% shorter dash cooldown | 5 |

Upgrade prices per level: 40, 90, 160, 250 and 360 coins.

## Menus and settings

- **Main menu**: Play, Shop, Settings and How to play (this guide).
- **Pause menu**: Resume, Settings, Quit to menu.
- **Game over**: your score, a "New best" notice, time survived and coins earned, with Retry, Shop and Menu.
- **Settings**: Sound effects, Music and Vibration, each on or off. They are remembered.

## Look and sound

- Hand-drawn style boats, islands, ice, plastic, sea life and enemies, with wake foam, island ripples, explosions and screen shake.
- Day and night cycle with sunset, plus rain, storms with lightning flashes, and fog.
- All sound is generated in the game: explosions, splashes, coins, power-ups, dash, cannon shots, thunder, warnings, boss roar, the engine hum and the music (a calm tune normally, a faster and lower one in boss fights).
- Made for phones: large touch buttons, a fullscreen portrait layout that stays clear of notches, and vibration on hits.
